The Story

My years of writing in film and television, theater, and fiction shaped my creative approach to Content Design at Microsoft. From ensuring language is people-focused and inclusive, to designing personality and dialogue for Cortana and Microsoft's bot framework, I'm living a career path I could never have anticipated.

Now, I find myself in the middle of an essential conversation—responsible approaches to designing for LLM-powered experiences—and I am honored to be a part of it. I'm passionate about bringing like-minded talent from the Arts and Humanities to technology. We need more people paying attention to the question of what it means to be human.
